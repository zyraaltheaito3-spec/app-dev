# My Favorite anime series: Demon Slayer

---

## Overview
**Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba** is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by *Koyoharu Gotouge*.  
It follows **Tanjiro Kamado**, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered and his sister **Nezuko** is turned into a demon.

---

## Characters
- **Tanjiro Kamado** – A determined boy with a strong sense of justice and compassion.  
- **Nezuko Kamado** – Tanjiro’s sister who fights to retain her humanity despite being a demon.  
- **Zenitsu Agatsuma** – A cowardly but powerful swordsman who fights using Thunder Breathing.  
- **Inosuke Hashibira** – A wild and fearless fighter who wears a boar mask.  

---

## Why is it my favorite
1. I love how the main character (**Tanjiro Kamado**) love his sister.
2. I like the plot and the fighting scene.
3. The animation quality is beautiful.
